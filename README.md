# Report_Sales_PowerBI
🚀 Sales Dashboard Project | Data Analytics Portfolio
Sharing a more detailed look at my Sales Dashboard, designed to provide a 360° view of business performance and help stakeholders make informed, data-driven decisions 📊
🔍 Key Highlights:
• Total Sales: 401K+ with a Profit of 68K+
• Profit Margin Analysis for performance tracking
• Sales breakdown by Category, Month, Payment Mode, and Sale Type
• Product-level insights with quantity and revenue contribution
• Clear visualization of trends and patterns to support strategic decisions

💡 This dashboard helps identify:
✔️ Top-performing categories and products
✔️ Sales trends across months
✔️ Customer purchasing behavior (Cash vs Online)
✔️ Business opportunities to optimize profit and cost
